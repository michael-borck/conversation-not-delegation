- **File:** `comic-strip/when-the-loop-runs-itself.png`
- **Prompt:**

  > Black-and-white hand-drawn stick-figure comic strip, three panels left to right, simple clean line art on a white background, with a bold hand-lettered punchline along the bottom. Same visual style as the book's other chapter comics.
  >
  > Panel 1: A stick figure at a keyboard typing a short, vague instruction shown on screen — "GOAL: make it better. GO!" — then turning to walk toward a bed, looking proud, a small "zzz" forming above its head.
  >
  > Panel 2: Night-time, a moon in the corner. A small round robot runs inside a loop of circular arrows, gears turning, a counter labelled "TOKENS $$$" ticking upward, cheerfully polishing an object. No human present. A thought bubble from the robot shows one big confident tick.
  >
  > Panel 3: Daytime. The stick figure returns, mouth open in dismay, facing a tall teetering stack of neat pages stamped "DONE ✓" — but the pages are visibly wrong (one shows a crooked, broken diagram).
  >
  > Bottom punchline caption (bold hand-lettering): "A LOOP IS ONLY AS GOOD AS THE TEST IT CAN'T TALK ITS WAY PAST."

### 08 — Is Prompting Dead? *(chapter retitled; regenerate to match the new theme — magic words vs clear instruction)*
- **File:** `comic-strip/08-rtcf.png`
- **Prompt:**

  > Black-and-white hand-drawn stick-figure comic strip, three panels left to right, simple clean line art on a white background, with a bold hand-lettered punchline along the bottom. Same visual style as the book's other chapter comics.
  >
  > Panel 1: A stick figure wearing a pointed wizard's hat waves a magic wand at a desktop computer, a speech bubble crammed with incantations — "You are a WORLD-CLASS expert!! Take a deep breath!! I'll tip you $200!!". The computer screen shows a flat, mediocre result and a small "meh".
  >
  > Panel 2: An ordinary stick figure (no hat) calmly speaking to the same computer the way you would brief a colleague, a tidy speech bubble — "Here's the situation, here's what I need, here's the one constraint." The screen shows a clean, strong result with a small tick.
  >
  > Panel 3: The wizard's hat lies on the floor; the first figure stares down at it, surprised.
  >
  > Bottom punchline caption (bold hand-lettering): "PROMPTING ISN'T DEAD. THE MAGIC WORDS ARE."
